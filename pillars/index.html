Pillars of a Changing World

  • Simplification: In a world reaching its ecological limits, exponential GDP growth tightly coupled to exponential energy use may not continue forever. The end of this trendline would have likely consequences that are non-linear, difficult to predict, and may destabilize rigid top-down authority structures and brittle infrastructure.

  • Decentralization: Ahead of failing top-down authorities and brittle infrastructure, investing in tools and infrastructure that allow decentralized groups to make decisions, share resources, and create together.

  • Migration: Accelerating climate change and crumbling of status quo infrastructures, paired with transformational decentralized technological infrastructures, will lead to great migrations of people to places more comfortable, habitable, and arable.

  • Islands of Coherence: In a chaotic system far from equilibrium, small islands of coherence have the capacity to shift a system into a higher order. Through all of our activities we seek to create and up-regulate islands of coherence.
