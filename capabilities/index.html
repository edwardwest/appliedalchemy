  1. 01

    Strategy

    Venture, product and movement strategy, from first concept through positioning and scale.

    Seen in: Prometheus Fuels, Levangie Labs, Earthbanc, The BioFi Project, Consilience Project

    We work alongside founders and leadership teams to decide what to build, who it is for, and why it wins: the venture concept, product strategy, market positioning, and the order of moves that takes an idea from a first conversation to scale.

    For movements and networks, strategy also means a theory of change and a plan for growing a community around it. Most often this is an embedded advisory role next to the CEO, covering strategy alongside communications, fundraising and business development.

  2. 02

    Feasibility

    Technical, financial and site feasibility worked out before capital is committed, including cost estimates and concept designs.

    Seen in: Tesseract, Terraform Robotics

    Before money goes into land, tooling or a build, we test whether the idea holds up: the engineering and technical risks, the costs and the financial model, and the conditions on the site itself.

    The result is something a decision can be made on: cost estimates, concept designs, and a clear list of what has to be true for the project to work.
